Buying

Original paintings

  • Each painting is one of a kind. I do not paint to order.
  • Paintings on canvas board have painted edges. They are varnished and ready to hang.
  • Every original is signed and comes with a certificate of authenticity.
  • Each original from The Shell series comes with the real shell it was painted from.
  • To buy a painting, use the "Buy original" link under it. You pay by card on a Square payment page.
  • Shipping in the US has a fixed price by size: $15 for small works (6 × 6 and 9 × 12 in), $45 for medium works (18 × 18 and 18 × 24 in), $150 for large paintings (48 × 30 in).
  • I pack and ship each painting myself from Melbourne, Florida.
  • For shipping outside the US, please write to me first.

Prints

  • Prints are made to order and shipped by Printful, my print partner.
  • Use the print links under a painting. You pay on the print shop page.
  • Prints ship to US addresses only.
